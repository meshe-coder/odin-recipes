Githeri Recipe

Githeri is a traditional Kenyan dish made with boiled maize and beans, often seasoned with spices and vegetables.

STEPS

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups of maize (corn)
  • 1 cup of beans (red kidney beans or any preferred type)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon of curry powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Vegetable oil for cooking

Instructions:

  1. Boil the maize and beans in separate pots until tender.
  2. In a large pan, heat vegetable oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and sauté until golden brown.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes, green bell pepper, and minced garlic. Cook for a few minutes until the vegetables are soft.
  4. Sprinkle in the curry powder, salt, and pepper. Stir well to combine.
  5. Add the boiled maize and beans to the pan. Mix everything together gently.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat for about 10-15 minutes, or until everything is well heated and seasoned.
  7. Once done, serve hot with your favorite side dishes or salad.
